### ALGO-000182: EFX existence for additive goods
- **Subfield:** Algorithmic fair division
- **Open:** Prove that every finite collection of indivisible goods with additive nonnegative valuations admits a complete envy-free-up-to-any-good allocation, or construct a counterexample.
- **Status:** Open since the modern EFX formulation. Three-agent existence is known; arbitrary numbers of agents remain unresolved.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Would establish a robust fairness guarantee without discarding goods.
- **Key works:** Caragiannis et al., *The Unreasonable Fairness of Maximum Nash Welfare*; Chaudhury, Garg, and Mehlhorn (2020), *EFX Exists for Three Agents*; Dagstuhl fair-division report.
- **Related:** Economics—existence of fair indivisible allocations.
- **Aliases:** Envy-freeness up to any good.
